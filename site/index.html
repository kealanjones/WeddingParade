Sop and Bop's Wedding Balloon Parade

A balloon from every visitor

At home, Sop and Bop have a chest of drawers that every visitor paints a hot-air balloon on. Each one is a little memento, and the chest keeps on growing.

For the wedding we're taking the tradition into the sky. Draw a balloon, any shape you like, and it will float in a parade alongside everyone else's.
