Sop and Bop's Wedding Balloon Parade

add yours!

Everyone who visits Sop and Bop at their home in Clyde Park paints a hot-air balloon on their chest of drawers. For the wedding, we'd love you to make one too.

  1. Draw a hot-air balloon on plain white paper, with lots of colour
  2. Take a photo of it on this page (or draw one on screen instead)
  3. Add your name and a note for the happy couple

Please send yours by midday on Thursday 15 October

On the wedding day, every balloon floats off the drawers and out over the bridge in one big parade, and your note goes with it.
